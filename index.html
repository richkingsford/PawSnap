A single ordinary photo has no absolute scale. A valid reference enables perspective-correct measurements, but accuracy still depends on corner placement, reference measurements, image resolution, lens distortion, and a flat surface. Off-plane beams, ducts and sloping sections need separate calibration. The sample photos alone cannot establish verified inch measurements.
